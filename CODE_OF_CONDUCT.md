# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the Spotify AI Organizer project and its communities will be treated with respect and dignity.

## Our Standards

We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, and inclusive community. Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** of different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- **Offering and accepting constructive feedback** gracefully
- **Focusing on communication** rather than personal attacks
- **Showing empathy** toward other community members
- **Helping others** learn and grow
- **Celebrating successes** and learning from failures together

## Unacceptable Behavior

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary behavior of any kind. Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination based on any protected characteristic
- Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuroatypicality, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion
- Unwelcome sexual attention or comments
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without consent
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above behavior
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior can be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement. All reports will be reviewed and investigated.

For reports, please email: **conduct@spotifyorganizer.dev**

All reports will be handled confidentially. We are committed to protecting the identity of the reporter.

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

1. **Warning** — A verbal warning for minor violations
2. **Temporary Ban** — Temporary removal from community spaces (7–30 days)
3. **Permanent Ban** — Permanent removal from community spaces for serious or repeated violations

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All community spaces (GitHub, Discord, forums, events)
- Official project spaces
- Public spaces where an individual is representing the project or community
